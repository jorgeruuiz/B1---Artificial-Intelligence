Healthcare

 Diagnosis

Ophthalmology (diabetic retinopathy): Deep CNNs on retinal fundus photos. Google's system (published in JAMA, 2016) matched specialists, and IDx-DR became the rst FDA-authorized
autonomous diagnostic AI in 2018.
Dermatology: Esteva et al. (Nature, 2017) trained a CNN to classify skin lesions at dermatologist level from photographs.
Radiology: CheXNet (a DenseNet) for pneumonia on chest X-rays; Google's mammography model (Nature, 2020) for breast cancer screening; Viz.ai's FDA-cleared stroke (large-vessel
occlusion) detection on CT.
Pathology: CNNs on whole-slide histology for detecting lymph-node metastases (the CAMELYON challenge); Paige Prostate became an FDA-authorized cancer-detection tool. Newer work
uses vision transformers and pathology foundation models.
Cardiology: ECG deep learning -- Stanford's arrhythmia model and Mayo Clinic's ECG-AI, which infers things not visible to humans, like reduced ejection fraction or atrial brillation from a
normal-rhythm ECG.

Healthcare

 Treatment development

Protein structure -- AlphaFold (DeepMind): The landmark example. AlphaFold2 (2021) solved structure prediction using attention-based architecture (the Evoformer); AlphaFold3 (2024)
extended this to protein­ligand and protein­nucleic-acid complexes. Related: RoseTTAFold and Meta's ESMFold (a protein language model).
Antibiotic discovery: Stokes et al. (Cell, 2020) used a graph neural network to screen millions of molecules and identi ed halicin, a structurally novel antibiotic.
Generative drug design: VAEs, GANs, and diffusion models for de novo molecule generation. Insilico Medicine used generative AI to design a brosis drug candidate that advanced into
clinical trials.
Genomics: DeepVariant (Google) for variant calling, SpliceAI for splice-site prediction, and Enformer for predicting gene expression from DNA sequence.

Healthcare

 Monitoring

EHR-based risk prediction: Rajkomar et al. (npj Digital Medicine, 2018) applied deep learning to raw electronic health records to predict mortality, readmission, and length of stay.
Sepsis early warning: Duke's Sepsis Watch and Johns Hopkins' TREWS, the latter validated in a large real-world deployment (Nature Medicine, 2022) showing faster treatment.
Wearables: Apple Watch atrial- brillation detection uses deep learning on photoplethysmography (PPG) signals; similar approaches drive continuous glucose prediction and fall detection.
Critical care: Models predicting hypotension or clinical deterioration from ICU vitals streams.
Neurology: Deep learning on EEG for seizure detection and automated sleep staging from wearable signals.
