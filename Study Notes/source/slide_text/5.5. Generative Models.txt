=== PDF PAGE 13 ===
Deep Learning && Generative
●Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN)
○Supervised … but “weakly”
○Known to be very hard (tricky) to train
○Generator stops learning (e.g. If the discriminator is too good,
gradients to the generator can vanish)
○Generator might slip up and get caught in a “mode collapse” (e.g.
finds a few outputs that perform well at fooling the discriminator, and
outputs only those for all inputs)
○Training procedure issues (The vanilla GAN setup doesn’t converge,
wild oscillations during training)
○Many strategies proposed to improve these issues
