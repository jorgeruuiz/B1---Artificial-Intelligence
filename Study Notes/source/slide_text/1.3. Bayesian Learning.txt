Learning hyperparameters

 Kernel hyperparameters are nonlinear.

    No closed-form. No Gaussian...

 We can approximate the solution!

    Empirical Bayes (type-II ML):
       MLE (or MAP) for the kernel hyperparameters.
       Warning: Underestimate uncertainty.
       Expensive.
       Similar alternative: Cross-validation.

    Sampling (e.g.: MCMC ­ next lesson):
       Full Bayesian
       Result in a mixture of GPs
       Much, much more expensive
